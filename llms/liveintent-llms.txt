# LiveIntent

> LiveIntent is a people-based email marketing and identity platform serving 2,500+ businesses. It connects marketers and publishers through email-native audience targeting, inventory monetization, and identity resolution across the digital ecosystem. LiveIntent has agreed to be acquired by Zeta Global. The company's primary public developer surface is the open-source LiveConnect first-party identity library rather than a REST API.

## Products
- People-based email marketing and audience targeting for marketers
- Email and web inventory monetization for publishers (LiveTag, native ad blueprints)
- Identity resolution and data solutions (HIRO, email reactivation)
- LiveConnect first-party identity collection for the browser
